Recording and measurement notes

Record from the tested side at hip height. Show the full leg and include at least half a second at rest before the first raise. Keep original capture timing. Select 120 or 240 fps for new recordings when available.

Thigh ROM measures hip-to-knee elevation relative to rest. Knee flexion comes from the hip, knee and ankle in the image. Shank ROM and speed are the closest video comparison when the IMU is mounted on the shin. Quality checks flag detectable tracking problems. Review the overlay to catch misplaced joints.
